Our story Half a century in the ground

“You’re only as strong as your weakest link.”

Dennis Frederickson · Co-Founder & Director

Founded by Neil Smith and Dennis Frederickson. Neil brought more than 50 years in excavation, heavy civil and agricultural work, from small backfills to highway jobs with crews of 50 or more. Dennis brought over 30 years in residential and commercial construction and a practical command of project management.

Today we are known for quality, cost control, schedule management and safety, on work ranging from utility upgrades and bulk excavation to specialized builds like the Manitoba Yard Fuel Station.

In memory of Neil Smith. His standards still set how we work on every site.
